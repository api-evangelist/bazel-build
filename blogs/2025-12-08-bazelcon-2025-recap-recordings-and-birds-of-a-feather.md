Autumn is BazelCon season, and this year was no different: Last month we hosted BazelCon 2025 together with the Linux Foundation and our sponsors. We were able to welcome 331 attendees from 214 organizations to Atlanta, Georgia. The conference kicked off with a day of sponsored sessions on Sunday, followed by two days filled with conference talks, birds-of-a-feather sessions and multiple unconferences.
